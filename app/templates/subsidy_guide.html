FAQ

Common subsidy questions

How much central subsidy is available for a 1 kW system?

₹30,000 under the published central CFA structure. Final approval depends on eligibility and the official process.

What is the maximum central subsidy?

The published central CFA cap is ₹78,000.

Does Uttar Pradesh offer additional subsidy?

The published UPNEDA material indicates additional support, subject to current availability and verification.

Is the UP amount guaranteed?

No. The additional amount is conditional and must be verified through current official scheme information.

Can commercial buildings receive residential PM Surya Ghar subsidy?

No automatic residential CFA eligibility applies to commercial or industrial systems.

Are off-grid and hybrid systems eligible?

Neither is automatically eligible. Eligibility must be verified against the latest official portal rules.

Where do I apply and when is subsidy released?

Apply on the PM Surya Ghar national portal. Disbursement timing and release depend on approval, feasibility, installation, commissioning and document verification.

Can I apply through Solar Setu?

No. Solar Setu provides guidance and estimates; applications and approvals remain with the official government portal and authorities.
